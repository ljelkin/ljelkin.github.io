about

I'm a philosopher working on AI, decision theory, epistemology, ethics, and the philosophy of cognitive science.

Much of my research asks how to reason under disagreement and uncertainty. Specifically, how to pool the judgments of many people, or many models, into a single view, and what pragmatic commitments that view carries.

Before joining HKU, I held postdoctoral positions at The Alan Turing Institute, Erasmus University Rotterdam, Jagiellonian University, and the University of Paris.
